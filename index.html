02The lighter car

Why the Corolla usually sips less fuel.

The Corolla is built as a compact sedan. It carries less mass, runs a smaller standard engine, and pushes less air aside at highway speed. Those three facts add up to lower fuel use for most commuters.

Three reasons the Corolla tends to win on raw fuel economy

  • It weighs less, so the engine works less to get moving.
  • Its standard gas engine is smaller in displacement than the Camry's powertrain.
  • A Corolla Hybrid version is offered for buyers chasing the highest possible MPG.
If your priority is the lowest fuel bill on a short city commute, the Corolla is the default answer.

It is also the lower entry price of the two, though we keep all pricing on our finance page at toyotadalton.com rather than in an article that can age.

03The hybrid midsize

Why the Camry stays competitive on efficiency.

The Camry is bigger, yet it is not the fuel hog the size difference might suggest. The current Camry generation went hybrid across the lineup, which narrows the gap with the Corolla considerably. You get midsize room and a hybrid system working together.

Most buyers assume a bigger car always means a much bigger fuel bill. Counterintuitively, a hybrid Camry can post efficiency numbers that surprise shoppers who expected a wide gap. The Camry will rarely beat a Corolla Hybrid on pure MPG, but it closes the distance while giving you more seat space and trunk room. 05The decision

How to pick between them for fuel and life.

Fuel consumption is one input, not the whole decision. Use these questions to narrow it down.

  • Commute length: short city trips favor the Corolla; long mixed-route drives narrow the gap.
  • Passengers and cargo: regular rear-seat passengers or big loads point to the Camry.
  • Budget: the Corolla typically carries the lower starting price.
  • Long-term efficiency: a hybrid version of either car raises its MPG ceiling.

Toyota's reputation for dependability sits behind both cars. Consumer Reports and J.D. Power both rate Toyota highly for reliability among mainstream brands, which is one reason these two sedans hold their value. You can read third-party owner and reliability data at kbb.com and cross-check ratings at consumerreports.org.

Every new Toyota also carries Toyota Safety Sense as standard driver-assist tech, so neither sedan asks you to trade safety features for fuel savings.

08Fuel questions

Frequently asked questions about Camry vs Corolla fuel.

Does the Corolla or Camry use less fuel?

The Corolla usually uses less fuel because it is lighter and runs a smaller standard engine. The current hybrid-only Camry narrows that gap, but a Corolla Hybrid still tends to post the highest MPG of the pair.

Check exact 2026 estimates at fueleconomy.gov.

Is the Camry still a fuel-efficient choice if it is bigger?

Yes. The current Camry generation is hybrid across the lineup, which keeps its fuel use competitive despite its larger size. You get midsize room with a hybrid system working to control consumption.

Confirm the current trim figures at fueleconomy.gov before buying.

Where can I find the exact MPG for each model?

The federal database at fueleconomy.gov lists EPA estimates by year, engine, and trim, and Toyota lists specs at toyota.com.

We do not print MPG figures here because they change by model year and a stale number misleads buyers. Ask us for current Toyota specs on your exact car.

Should I buy a Corolla or a Camry for a long commute?

It depends on your priorities. A long commute with one or two people favors the Corolla for lower fuel cost, while a commute with passengers or cargo favors the Camry's space.

Both are reliable Toyota sedans, so the choice comes down to room versus running cost.

Are the Corolla and Camry reliable?

Consumer Reports and J.D. Power both rate Toyota highly for reliability among mainstream brands, and both the Corolla and Camry benefit from that record. You can review third-party reliability data at consumerreports.org and kbb.com.

Reliability is one reason these sedans tend to hold their value well.
